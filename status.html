The federal Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (SCRA, 50 U.S.C. § 3937) caps interest on a servicemember's PRE-SERVICE obligations at 6% per annum during military service (and one additional year for mortgage loans), upon the servicemember's written request and a copy of military orders (or creditor verification). 18 U.S.C. §§ 891–896 (Extortionate Credit Transactions Act) establishes federal criminal usury liability. Under 18 U.S.C. § 892, an extension of credit is presumed extortionate if: (1) it is unenforceable under state civil usury laws; (2) the annual rate of interest exceeds 45% (calculated under § 891(5) including all fees, service charges, discounts, and premiums); and (3) the debtor reasonably believed extortionate means were used or the creditor has a reputation for violence/extortion. 18 U.S.C. § 894 criminalizes collection of credit by extortionate means (violence, threats).
